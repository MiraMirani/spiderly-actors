## linkedin

Create a NEW square app-icon design using the two images as references. Reference 1 supplies the exact Spiderly mascot identity: purple friendly round spider, huge white eyes with dark plum rims, pink cheeks, two little white teeth and curved plum legs. Reference 2 supplies the blue LinkedIn 'in' platform mark. Match the original mascot closely and keep its flat vector-like appearance. Background MUST be one perfectly solid, uniform pale pink #FFE3EC filling the entire square, opaque from edge to edge. Absolutely no transparency, gradients, shadows, fog, glow or vignettes. Keep the mascot large and central, as in reference 1, and add the LinkedIn mark inside a white rounded-square badge in the bottom-right, approximately 30% of the canvas width, with a clean dark plum outline. The badge may cover the bottom-right outer legs, but never the eyes or mouth. Keep all artwork within the canvas with 4% safe margins. Preserve LinkedIn's familiar blue-and-white 'in' graphic faithfully. No labels, no titles, no watermark. One finished square icon, not a sheet of icons.
